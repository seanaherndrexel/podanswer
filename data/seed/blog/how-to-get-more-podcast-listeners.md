The average podcast gets fewer than 100 downloads per episode in its first month. Most hosts hear that and think they're failing. They're not. They just haven't picked a channel that compounds.

## Spikes versus compounding

A social post brings a spike. Fifty people click, a handful subscribe, and by Thursday it's over. You have to do it again next week to get the same fifty.

A page that ranks on Google brings a trickle that never stops. Ten readers a day, every day, for as long as it ranks. Write forty of those pages and you have four hundred readers a day arriving without you doing anything that morning.

Growth that lasts comes from stacking compounding channels, not from finding a bigger spike.

## The three channels that compound

**Search.** Written answers to real questions, sourced from your episodes, indexed by Google. This is the one most podcasters never touch because it feels like a different job. It's the reason PodAnswer exists.

**Guest appearances.** Every show you appear on keeps sending listeners from its back catalog for years.

**Your own back catalog.** Once a listener finds one episode, good titles and show notes send them to five more. Retitle old episodes as questions and watch the long tail wake up.

## A realistic curve

Here's what compounding looks like for an educational show that publishes weekly and gets its search channel running:

- Months 1 to 3: articles get indexed. A few start ranking for long, specific questions. Small numbers.
- Months 4 to 6: dozens of pages ranking. Readers per day climb past what any social post delivers.
- Months 7 to 12: the hub has authority in its subject. New articles rank in days instead of weeks. Listener growth becomes steady and predictable.

Nothing about that requires luck or a viral moment.

## What to measure

Downloads per episode in the first seven days is the standard number, but it lags. The leading indicators are: how many pages you have ranking, how many readers those pages get, and how many of them click through to listen. If those three go up, downloads follow a month later.
